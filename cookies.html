Digital Privacy Architecture

Cookie Policy & Preference Management

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Clear Transparency on Storage & Web Technologies

1. What Are Cookies and Local Storage Technologies?

Cookies are compact text files placed upon your computer, mobile device, or tablet when you navigate modern websites. These small files enable web platforms to recognize your browser, retain user configuration preferences (such as selected meal planning parameters or botanical flavor swatches), and ensure smooth navigational transitions. In addition to HTTP cookies, modern web browsers utilize Local Storage and Session Storage mechanisms to maintain client-side interface state without transmitting redundant server data.

2. How ZestyMealHut Employs Storage Technologies

We maintain a philosophy of minimal data footprint. We employ cookies and local browser storage strictly to deliver a refined, responsive culinary digital experience and evaluate aggregated website performance:

A. Strictly Essential Cookies & Local State

These elements are fundamental to website functionality and cannot be deactivated in our systems. They manage:

  • Preservation of your cookie consent status (so the banner does not repeatedly disrupt your reading).
  • Local caching of your active selections within the interactive Meal Flavor & Macro Architect and Botanical Herb Harmonizer.
  • Security tokens preventing cross-site request forgery during contact and reservation form submissions.

B. Performance & Analytical Measurement

We utilize Google Analytics (Tag ID: G-0LY0HY7L01) to understand how readers engage with our culinary monographs and recipe tools. These cookies track:

  • Aggregated page visit counts, reading durations, and bounce metrics.
  • General device types and screen resolutions to ensure optimal responsive layout scaling.
  • Referral pathways through which readers discover our culinary monographs.
  • All analytics data is collected anonymously with IP masking enabled, ensuring no connection to identifiable guest profiles.

C. Functional Experience Preferences

These technologies remember your browsing preferences, such as preferred typographic sizing or mobile navigation states, ensuring a customized and seamless aesthetic experience during subsequent visits.

4. Advanced Local Storage & Session State Engineering

In addition to standard HTTP cookies, ZestyMealHut leverages contemporary HTML5 Web Storage protocols (including localStorage and sessionStorage) to provide seamless client-side computational capabilities without requiring continuous server queries. When you adjust the parameters within our interactive Meal Flavor & Macro Architect or select specialized herbs in the Botanical Harmonizer, these state configurations are processed directly inside your browser engine.

This architecture provides two substantial benefits: first, it guarantees lightning-fast, zero-latency responsiveness when toggling between seasonal tasting menus; second, it ensures that your personalized culinary configurations remain strictly contained within your local device sandboxing, never transmitting unnecessary personal behavioral breadcrumbs across external network nodes.

5. Do Not Track (DNT) and Global Privacy Control (GPC) Compliance

Our digital infrastructure recognizes and respects modern browser privacy signals, including standard Do Not Track (DNT) headers and Global Privacy Control (GPC) automation flags. When your browser transmits a verified DNT or GPC signal, our systems automatically suppress all non-essential performance tracking and analytical telemetry, strictly confining our operations to fundamental functional scripts necessary to render typography and interactive culinary widgets.

6. How to Manage and Deactivate Cookie Settings

You possess complete authority over cookie preferences. You can adjust your choices through multiple methods:

  • Our Cookie Banner: Use the interactive cookie banner at the bottom of our pages to accept all or restrict to essential storage only.
  • Browser Controls: Most contemporary browsers (Google Chrome, Apple Safari, Mozilla Firefox, Microsoft Edge) allow you to block, delete, or inspect cookies via their privacy settings. Please note that disabling essential cookies may impact the interactivity of our recipe configurators.

7. Updates to this Cookie Policy

We may periodically update this policy to incorporate technological upgrades or statutory updates. Revisions take effect immediately upon publication.
