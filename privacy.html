Digital Privacy Architecture

Privacy Policy & Guest Data Stewardship

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • Framework for Culinary Integrity & Digital Protection

1. The Commitment to Guest Discretion and Trust

At ZestyMealHut, accessible via zestymealhut.com ("the Kitchen", "we", "us", or "our"), safeguarding the discretion, personal records, and dietary preferences of our dining guests and digital readers is a sacred operational foundation. Whether you explore our wild fermentation monographs, utilize our interactive Meal Flavor & Macro Architect, reserve seats at our Chef's Tasting Table, or join the VIP Tasting Register, we handle your personal information with strict administrative discipline and modern cryptographic security.

This Privacy Policy provides full transparency regarding how we collect, process, manage, and safeguard your personal details, dietary records, and automated digital telemetry across our web portal, mobile interfaces, and in-person culinary salon interactions.

2. Categories of Information We Collect

We collect distinct classes of information to coordinate bespoke culinary dining, customize seasonal tasting menus, and optimize web performance:

A. Guest Contact & Reservation Information

When you reserve a table, submit a private catering request, or join our culinary register, we collect:

  • Full legal name, formal title, and honorific preferences.
  • Electronic mail address and primary telephonic contact number.
  • Physical residence or corporate event locations for private bespoke hearth catering.
  • Billing contact details and payment confirmation tokens processed through encrypted third-party payment gateways.

B. Dietary Preferences & Allergen Profiles

To ensure guest wellness and culinary precision during multi-course tasting menus, we may record:

  • Dietary lifestyle parameters (e.g., Mediterranean, plant-based, paleo, celiac-safe).
  • Specific botanical sensitivities, lactose tolerances, or severe food allergen disclosures.
  • Historical tasting preferences, favorite seasonal ingredients, and beverage pairing notes.

C. Automated Technical Telemetry & Browser Metadata

As you navigate our culinary platform, automated systems passively collect technical parameters:

  • Internet Protocol (IP) address and approximate geographic location (city and country level).
  • Browser classification, operating platform, device architecture, and screen viewport dimensions.
  • Page view sequences, dwell durations, referring URLs, and interactive tool usage (such as the Meal Flavor Architect and Botanical Herb Harmonizer).

3. Legal Grounds and Operational Purposes for Data Processing

We process your personal and dietary data strictly under legitimate legal frameworks recognized by global data protection statutes (including GDPR and CCPA / CPRA):

  • Contractual Execution: To confirm Chef's Table bookings, procure rare morning farm harvests for your party, and execute customized dining experiences.
  • Legitimate Kitchen Interests: To improve web infrastructure, conduct scientific analyses on recipe performance, protect against reservation fraud, and maintain kitchen safety standards.
  • Express Guest Consent: To transmit seasonal harvest announcements, private chef monograph releases, and culinary event invitations when explicitly requested.
  • Statutory & Health Compliance: To comply with municipal food safety record-keeping mandates, tax accounting statutes, and consumer protection laws.

4. Protection of Digital Records & Encryption Architecture

We implement multi-layered technological and physical defense protocols to shield your information from unauthorized access, accidental alteration, disclosure, or destruction. All data transmission across zestymealhut.com is fortified with Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3) protocols with 256-bit cryptographic cipher suites.

Guest dietary profiles and reservation archives are sequestered within isolated, encrypted databases protected by role-based access controls. Access is strictly limited to authorized culinary managers, executive chefs, and senior concierge staff.

5. Third-Party Service Providers and Data Isolation

ZestyMealHut maintains an uncompromising standard: we never sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal information or dietary profiles to third-party data brokers or advertisers.

We partner only with vetted technological and operational providers who conform to strict data confidentiality agreements:

  • Hosting & Content Infrastructure: Secure cloud infrastructure partners providing resilient, high-speed content delivery.
  • Analytics Providers: We employ Google Analytics (Tracking ID: G-0LY0HY7L01) using IP anonymization to evaluate aggregate traffic patterns without correlating data to identified individual patrons.
  • Payment Gateways: PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant processors handling end-to-end encrypted financial transactions without storing raw card numbers on our servers.

6. International Data Transfers & Global Safeguards

As an international culinary destination welcoming travelers worldwide, your data may be processed on secure servers located in the United States. Whenever we process records originating from the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom, or Switzerland, we implement Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) to ensure equivalent levels of protection.

7. Data Retention Protocols

We retain your personal records and communication history only for the duration required to achieve the operational purposes outlined in this policy or to satisfy statutory accounting requirements. Dietary profiles are preserved across seasonal cycles to allow ongoing personalized hospitality, unless you instruct us to delete them.

8. Your Statutory Privacy Rights

Depending on your geographic residency, you possess comprehensive legal rights regarding your personal records:

  • Right of Access: You may request a complete transcript of the personal records and dietary metrics we hold concerning your account.
  • Right of Rectification: You may correct inaccurate or outdated contact information, dietary notes, or address details at any time.
  • Right of Erasure ("Right to Be Forgotten"): You may instruct us to permanently purge your digital profiles, reservation history, and dietary notes, subject to statutory retention obligations.
  • Right to Restrict Processing: You may limit how we process your information during active disputes or reviews.
  • Right to Data Portability: You may request your dietary data and tasting history in a structured, machine-readable format.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You can unsubscribe from our tasting gazette or withdraw cookie consents at any time without prejudice.

9. Protection of Minor Privacy

ZestyMealHut is a culinary destination tailored for adults aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly solicit, collect, or retain data from individuals under the age of 16 without verified parental consent. If we discover that personal information of a minor has been submitted, we take immediate measures to delete such records from our servers.

10. Revisions to this Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect advancements in digital security, modifications in kitchen operations, or evolutions in global privacy jurisprudence. Any updates will be published on this page accompanied by a revised effective date. We encourage guests to review this document regularly.

12. Allergen Discretion and Cross-Contamination Security Protocols

Given the vital importance of food allergy management in contemporary culinary environments, ZestyMealHut implements specialized digital controls governing guest dietary profiles. When you communicate severe botanical sensitivities, celiac dietary parameters, or tree nut allergies through our digital reservation portal, this data is classified as high-priority confidential information.

We do not aggregate or cross-reference guest allergy records across external public directories. Your dietary considerations are retained exclusively within our internal culinary preparation terminal to enable our kitchen brigade to execute sanitized mise-en-place, dedicate sterilized cast-iron cookware, and safeguard your dining health without exposing personal information to external commercial entities.

13. Guest Telemetry and Digital Interface Optimization

To ensure that our interactive culinary tools—including the Meal Flavor & Macro Architect and the Botanical Herb Harmonizer—perform with zero latency across desktop and mobile devices, we collect aggregated interface telemetry. This includes assessing browser rendering speeds of recipe charts and tracking interface engagement times to refine layout responsiveness. All such telemetry is stripped of individual identifying attributes and processed solely in aggregate format.
