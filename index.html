The Kitchen Craft

The 4-Step Hearth Cookery Protocol

How our executive chefs guide raw ingredients from the morning farm harvest to golden-crusted perfection.

1

Soil-to-Kitchen Intake

Produce harvested at peak diurnal sugar concentration, washed in spring water, and prepped without industrial peeling.

2

Enzymatic Brining

Meats and dense vegetables submerged in cold herbal mineral brines with fresh bay leaves, peppercorns, and Meyer lemon.

3

Direct Ember Sear

Flash-charred over radiant 800°F oak embers to instantly seal internal juices and caramelize natural sugars.

4

Resting & Emulsion

Resting on warm cedar boards before finishing with first cold-pressed polyphenol olive oil and flaked sea salt.

Frequently Answered

Culinary & Kitchen Inquiries

Essential information regarding private tasting reservations, dietary accommodations, and sourdough fermentation.

How does the Chef's Tasting Table reservation operate?

Our Chef's Tasting Table accommodates private parties of up to twelve guests right before our open-flame hearth. Reservations are released thirty days in advance through our digital concierge, featuring a bespoke seven-course progression tailored to seasonal morning farm harvests.

Can you accommodate specific dietary intolerances and sensitivities?

Yes. Because every dish is prepared from whole, unrefined ingredients from scratch, our kitchen effortlessly tailors tasting menus for celiac, dairy-free, vegan, and specific botanical sensitivities with 48-hour advance notice.

What makes your sourdough fermentation process unique?

We cultivate our wild sourdough starter using organic stone-milled heirloom grains. Our dough undergoes a 48-hour cold fermentation that neutralizes over 90% of phytic acid and allows lactobacilli to break down gluten proteins, making the bread exceptionally light, aromatic, and easy to digest.

Do you offer zero-proof pairing progressions?

Absolutely. Our in-house botanical lab crafts artisanal zero-proof beverage pairings—such as cold-pressed blood orange shrubs, sparkling rosemary tonics, and fermented heirloom apple ciders—designed to harmonize with each course.
